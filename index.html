01About

I am a Principal Researcher at Tencent, leading efforts on code agents for long-horizon tasks. I also collaborate closely with Prof. Atlas Wang.

I received my Ph.D. in 2025 from the NUS HPC-AI Lab, advised by Prof. Yang You. During my Ph.D., I was an affiliated member at the University of Toronto with Prof. Konstantinos N. Plataniotis, and a sponsored researcher at Carnegie Mellon University with Prof. Alex Hauptmann. Before that, I received my M.S. in 2020 from MMLab@SIAT, advised by Prof. Yu Qiao and Assoc. Prof. Xiaojiang Peng.

I like simple, strong baselines that reveal how deep learning actually works. Representative work: HY-WU, DnD (NeurIPS 2025), RPG (NeurIPS 2025), p-diff, and PAB (ICLR 2025).
